01 / System Direction

Building practical systems that connect real workflows with technical solutions.

HYDROFLO, or Hydrologic Drainage Automation and Flood-Level Optimizer, represents my early engineering automation direction. It explores flood-control planning through drainage automation, temporary rainwater storage, water-level monitoring, flood-gate support, and controlled pumping.

System Type Engineering automation concept

03 / Design Thinking

Workflow First

I start by understanding the real process, users, pain points, and service flow before thinking about the technical structure of the system.

Practical Implementation

My projects focus on systems that can be explained clearly, demonstrated practically, and connected to real academic or community-based problems.

Protected Development

Public pages describe the purpose, learning value, and general structure while keeping private code, architecture, and exact logic protected.
