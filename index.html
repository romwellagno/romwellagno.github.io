Developing my technical foundation through programming, systems, AI, and documentation.

I am currently developing my skills in Computer Engineering through programming fundamentals, Java development, Python experimentation, interface design, object-oriented thinking, system planning, engineering proposal writing, AI assistant development, and technical documentation.

My programming foundation started with C++, where I learned basic syntax, variables, conditionals, loops, functions, and problem-solving logic before expanding into Java, Python, HTML, and CSS.

One of my current major personal projects is Z.O.R.A., my own AI assistant concept. I am developing it in Visual Studio using Python, with Llama-based AI experimentation and a local development setup supported by Homebrew.

ZORA can now access the internet, respond to voice questions, and speak fluently. The project is still under active development, and public details are kept limited to protect the system direction and implementation approach.

Zettabyte Operations and Research Assistant

Z.O.R.A. stands for Zettabyte Operations and Research Assistant, a personal AI system developed by Romwell C. Agno to support voice interaction, internet-based research, academic assistance, and idea generation. It is designed as a practical student-built assistant for learning, productivity, research support, and experimental automation.

Built as part of Romwell’s journey in Computer Engineering, ZORA reflects curiosity, technical exploration, and the ambition to create an AI companion that is useful not only for answering questions, but also for organizing ideas, supporting academic work, and assisting in future projects.
