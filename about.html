01 / Profile

A practical learner shaped by service, operations, and technology.

My professional foundation began in hospitality, customer service, branch operations, and sales-driven workplace environments. Through these experiences, I developed a practical understanding of service flow, team coordination, customer experience, operational discipline, and performance-oriented management.

Over time, my work expanded into digital marketing, graphic design, social media management, CRM-related tasks, administrative support, and business analytics. This transition helped me connect my service-oriented background with digital tools, online systems, and technology-supported workflows.

I am now pursuing Computer Engineering to build a stronger technical foundation in programming, engineering proposals, system design, digital workflows, and project documentation. My goal is to develop practical systems that are useful, understandable, and connected to real operational and community-based needs.
