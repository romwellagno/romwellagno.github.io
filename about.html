Z.O.R.A. AI Assistant

ZORA is my personal AI assistant project and my own version of an AI system. It is being developed using Python, Visual Studio, Llama, and Homebrew, with current focus on voice interaction, internet-connected responses, fluent speech output, local experimentation, and assistant behavior.

The project can now access the internet, respond to voice questions, and speak fluently. The full implementation details, internal logic, and development approach are kept private while the project continues to evolve.

Z.O.R.A.

Zettabyte Operations and Research Assistant

Z.O.R.A. stands for Zettabyte Operations and Research Assistant, a personal AI system developed to support voice interaction, internet-based research, academic assistance, and idea generation. Inspired by fictional AI assistants such as J.A.R.V.I.S. and E.D.I.T.H., ZORA is designed as a practical student-built assistant for learning, productivity, and experimental automation.

Second Brain AI System

My Second Brain is a personal knowledge and learning workflow built using Llama, Copilot, and Obsidian as storage. It helps me process lecture-based information, create new ideas from my class materials, and understand subjects more effectively.

This system supports my academic learning by connecting lecture notes, subject explanations, and idea generation into one organized knowledge workflow.

Project Type Personal learning and knowledge system
Current Stack Llama, Copilot, Obsidian
Knowledge Base Obsidian as storage
Purpose Lecture understanding and idea generation
