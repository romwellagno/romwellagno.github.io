01 / Project Overview

A flood-control concept for safer and more resilient barangay infrastructure.

HYDROFLO is a Computer Engineering academic proposal that explores an integrated flood-control system for barangays located near coastal areas and rivers. The concept focuses on reducing flood risk through drainage automation, temporary rainwater storage, water-level sensing, and controlled water release.

The project was developed as a 2025 COED proposal and includes a miniature model concept to demonstrate how rainwater may be collected, stored, monitored, and released through a controlled pumping system.

02 / Schematic

Conceptual system layout.

The schematic presents the main parts of the proposed system, including sensors, a reservoir, flood gate, out-take pump, outlet pump, emergency outlet pump, and solar-assisted monitoring component.

03 / Objectives

Reduce Flooding Risk

The proposal aims to support flood-risk reduction in coastal and river-adjacent barangays through a structured drainage and storage concept.

Store Rainwater Temporarily

The system concept includes an underground tank designed for temporary rainwater storage during peak flooding events.

Control Water Release

The project integrates a pumping concept that allows collected water to be released in a controlled manner or reused for selected community purposes.

04 / System Components

Catchment Area 1 km × 6 m road concept
Rainwater Volume Based on average annual rainfall

Tank Concept Underground reservoir for controlled drainage
Pumping Concept Controlled release to rivers or coastal outlets
Safety Concept Backflow prevention and emergency outlet support
06 / Engineering Value

Community Safety

The concept supports disaster-preparedness thinking by focusing on flood-risk reduction and improved water management.

Sustainable Water Use

The proposal considers possible water reuse for community purposes such as irrigation or cleaning, depending on system conditions.

Responsible Engineering

The project connects engineering design with ethical, sustainable, and community-centered problem-solving.

07 / Learning Value

A proposal that connects engineering concepts with real community problems.

HYDROFLO helped develop my understanding of engineering proposal writing, infrastructure-based problem identification, system component planning, miniature model presentation, and responsible design for community-based challenges.

It also became an early Computer Engineering milestone because it required combining technical research, visual system planning, sustainability considerations, and practical presentation.
