01 / Project Overview

Flood-control concept for high-risk community areas.

HYDROFLO stands for Hydrologic Drainage Automation and Flood-Level Optimizer. It is a Computer Engineering academic proposal designed to explore how rainwater can be collected, temporarily stored, monitored, and released through a controlled pumping system.

The project concept focuses on reducing flood risk through drainage automation, temporary rainwater storage, water-level sensing, and controlled water release for barangays located near coastal areas and rivers.

The project was developed as a 2025 COED academic proposal and includes a miniature model concept to demonstrate how rainwater may be collected, stored, monitored, and released through a controlled pumping system.

02 / Schematic

Conceptual system layout.

The schematic presents the main parts of the proposed system, including sensors, a reservoir, flood gate, out-take pump, outlet pump, emergency outlet pump, and solar-assisted monitoring component.

03 / System Purpose
Engineering Proposal
Purpose Flood-Risk Reduction

Designed to demonstrate controlled rainwater handling.

The main purpose of HYDROFLO is to present a conceptual system that can collect excess rainwater, store it temporarily in an underground reservoir, monitor water levels, and release water through controlled pumps when conditions are safer.

The proposal is intended as an academic engineering concept and miniature model demonstration, not as a fully deployed municipal infrastructure system.

05 / System Flow

Rainwater Collection

Excess rainwater enters the drainage path and is directed toward a temporary storage area instead of immediately contributing to surface flooding.

Level Monitoring

Water-level sensors monitor the stored water level to support decision-making for controlled water release and emergency response.

Controlled Release

Pumps and outlet pathways are used conceptually to release stored water in a controlled manner when discharge conditions are safer.
